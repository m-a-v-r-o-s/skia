Privacy Policy

SKIÁ Athens ("SKIÁ", "we", "us") respects your privacy. This page explains what data we collect through this website and what we do with it.

What we collect

When you send an enquiry through the "Acquire" form, we collect the name, email address, and any notes you enter. We use this only to reply to your enquiry about a piece, a fitting, or a stockist. We do not sell or share this data with third parties for marketing.

Cookies and third parties

This site loads typefaces from Google Fonts and Fontshare, which may receive your IP address as part of that request. We do not set our own tracking or advertising cookies.

Contact
